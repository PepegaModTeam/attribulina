# game opens no Lua standard library.

# The E<Name> argument names are the events' fields in the GameCube build's debug header
# (exes/gamecube/mw-vanilla/NFSMWRELEASE.h). The Xbox build (exes/xbox/mw-vanilla/default.xbe) binds the same
# names and events; scripts/lua_api/scan_mw.py compares them (mw_builds.txt in its work folder).
# Signatures and descriptions of names Pro Street also binds come from data/lua/prostreet_pc.txt and were not
# checked against this game; the stub notes ("Does nothing on PC.") were. The E<Name> lines are written from
# this game's own definitions.
